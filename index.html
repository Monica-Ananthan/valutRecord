Why VaultRecords

Security built into every aisle

Six layers of protection on every box we hold.

  • Badge and PIN access

    Every entry is logged and reviewed monthly, so you always know who has been in.

  • Climate controlled

    Stable temperature and humidity for paper and media.

  • Fire suppression

    Water-free systems protect paper from damage.

  • 24/7 monitoring

    Recorded camera coverage of every storage area and loading bay.

  • Certified destruction

    Shredded only after your approval, with a certificate for every box.

  • Restricted key control

    Keys and cards are issued, tracked and revoked from one place.
