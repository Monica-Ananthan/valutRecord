Quick answers

Questions? Get answers and a quote

Read the essentials, then tell us what you need.

How do I request a box?

Sign in to the client portal, choose the box and pick a delivery option. Next-day delivery is included, and rush same-day delivery is available for an extra fee.

Can you scan a box instead of delivering it?

Yes. Request scanning for any box and the finished files appear in your Documents page. You choose whether the originals go back on the shelf or are destroyed.

How is pricing worked out?

Storage is charged per box per month, retrieval per box delivered and scanning per page. There is no setup fee on accounts over 100 boxes.

What happens when a retention period ends?

The box is listed in your portal for approval. Nothing is destroyed without it, and you receive a certificate afterwards.
