How do I request a box?

Sign in to the client portal, choose the box and pick a delivery option. Next-day delivery is included.

Can I get a same-day delivery?

Yes. Choose rush same-day delivery for an extra $18 per box.

How do I return a box?

Request a pickup in your portal and we scan the box back into storage.

What does it cost?

Storage is $0.38 per box per month, retrieval $6.50 per box and scanning $0.09 per page.

When am I invoiced?

Storage is invoiced monthly. Retrieval and scanning are invoiced once completed.

How do you keep records secure?

Badge and PIN access, camera monitoring, climate control, fire suppression and barcode tracking.

What happens when a retention period ends?

The box is listed for your approval. Nothing is destroyed without it, and you receive a certificate.

Which scan formats do you offer?

Searchable PDF with OCR, plain PDF or TIFF archive.

How long does scanning take?

Standard turnaround is 5 to 7 days, and rush turnaround is 2 days.
